# Breakage

- A transcript needs at least two full-name speakers with at least five labels each and at least twelve labels for those speakers in total. A quote must fit on one speech line before the last two speech turns. Another creditable name anywhere from the label through the quote blocks credit. If the layout inserts short lines or the snapshot cuts off labels, check rejected quotes against the saved transcript.
- A speaker can quote someone else's words in their own turn, for example "As you said, ...", and the match can still credit the current speaker. The extraction step is the only defense against that mix-up.
- Builder takes rely on saved page text and RSS summaries. A feed, article template or snapshot path change can hide the quote, byline or original date and reduce the number of published takes. Check the day's `items.json`, snapshots and publication rejections in `run.log`. When a page has multiple voices or dates, compare the extracted speaker and date to the article before publication.
- Byline matching for a source's own author (only sources with `take_byline: true`): when the speaker is the person the source is named after, the quote may sit anywhere on the saved page as long as the name is in the first 1,000 characters. If a page's saved text includes reader comments or long quoted passages, someone else's words could be credited to the author. Trigger: a source whose snapshots start including comment threads, or a link blog that is mostly quotes. Check: compare a sample of that source's takes with the live page; if it happens, remove `take_byline` from that source. Never turn it on for a link blog.
